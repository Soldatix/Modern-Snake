Modern Snake

SNAKE
Modern Edition

A fast, clean and modern take on the classic Snake. Eat, grow and climb the leaderboard.

⌨️ Use Arrow keys or WASD. On mobile, swipe or use the on-screen controls.
🎯 Goal: Eat the golden food, grow the snake and score as many points as possible.
❤️ Lives: You start with 3 lives. Hitting a wall or your own body costs one life.
⭐ Bonus: Temporary bonus food appears for a few seconds. Hearts give an extra life, stars give bonus points.
